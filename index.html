Matthew Hirabayashi, MD

Surgeon · Innovator · Educator

For Patients

Bio

Matt Hirabayashi, MD is a board-certified, fellowship-trained ophthalmologist specializing in vision correction and cataract surgery. He is an expert in EVO ICL, SMILE, LASIK, PRK, CTAK, and laser cataract surgery.

His work is centered on education, innovation, and excellence in refractive surgery, with a personal commitment to helping patients understand their possibilities and achieve their best vision.

Education

Matt Hirabayashi is the first author of the refractive surgery textbook used by WCRS/Refractive Foundations to educate fellows across the country and board member of the World Council of Refractive Surgery Fellowships. He also creates widely followed YouTube content for both surgeons and patients. Additionally, he is the youngest surgeon proctor for EVO ICL to teach and approve other surgeons in this technology.

Innovation

He has developed surgical instruments, educational tools for other surgeons, and software-based nomograms designed to improve surgical planning and outcomes. He is the only surgeon in San Antonio and Amarillo to offer every vision correction procedure including EVO ICL, SMILE, LASIK (WFO, Contoura, and Wavelight Plus), RLE (Lens Replacement), Femtosecond Laser-Assisted Cataract Surgery, and CTAK (Corneal Tissue Addition Keratoplasty).

Excellence

His dedication to excellence is reflected in his selection as Chief Resident, membership in Alpha Omega Alpha, and completion of a dedicated refractive surgery fellowship. Having undergone EVO ICL surgery himself and performed EVO ICL surgery for his wife, he brings a personal understanding to his work. He performs ICL surgery for patients from all walks of life and from across the country who seek his expertise. He was recognized by STAAR surgical in early 2026 for his experience and the amount of ICLs he has performed.
